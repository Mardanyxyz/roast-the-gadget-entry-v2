Entrants write a single original line of at most 200 characters that roasts a gadget or app they use every day: the phone that dies at four in the afternoon, the app that asks for a rating before it opens, the smart kettle that wants a firmware update first. A judge model compares entries two at a time on how funny the line is, how fresh the angle is and how neatly it is worded, and the best lines take the top three places.
